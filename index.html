FAQs

Good to know.

What can I do with PennyTree?

See balances and spending across linked Nigerian bank accounts, log cash entries, set budgets, and track financial goals.

Can I start without linking a bank?

Yes. You can add a manual account and track cash expenses yourself.

Is PennyTree a bank?

PennyTree helps you track and plan your finances. Your money stays in your bank accounts.

Which banks can I connect?

PennyTree uses Mono for bank connections. Check the in-app connection flow for the supported institutions available to you.
